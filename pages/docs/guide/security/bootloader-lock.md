You may have gotten a "Bootloader lock warning" message that brings you to this page (**calyxos.org/lock**)

Relocking, just like unlocking, wipes your device. Make sure you have everything you need before continuing, our backup solution, Seedvault, may help you.

Our device-flasher and web installer try to make sure that relocking is safe, and as such is the safest method to relock the bootloader, instead of doing it manually yourself.

Feel free to contact us at [[community]] for any questions or concerns.

## Pixel
It should be safe to relock the bootloader anytime.

## SHIFTphone
**DO NOT** relock the bootloader without additional checks!

## Fairphone
**DO NOT** relock the bootloader without additional checks!

See the [Fairphone relock](/fairphone-relock/) page.

## Motorola
**DO NOT** relock the bootloader without additional checks!

See the [Motorola relock](/motorola-relock/) page.
